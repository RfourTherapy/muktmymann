Collaboration · TEDxRambaug

Mental Health Check: #KaiseHo?

10 October 2021 · World Mental Health Day4:00 – 7:00 PMBookmark Cafe, Kalyan (W)

"Kaise ho?" (how are you?) is a question we ask every day without waiting for the answer. Together with TEDxRambaug, we turned it into an open mental health check-in: an afternoon of talks, honest sharing and community at Bookmark Cafe, open to everyone.

MannMukt completes 3 years

October 2022College campus

To mark three years of MannMukt, students and volunteers took to campus with handmade placards carrying one simple message: mental health is as real, and as important, as physical health.

Mental Health Awareness Meet

2025

An evening that began with lighting the lamp among marigolds and grew into a room full of stories. Talks on mental health were followed by an open sharing circle, a letter-to-self writing exercise, and handmade bookmarks for every guest with reminders like "It's okay not to be okay."
