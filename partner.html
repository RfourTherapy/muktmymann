Who we work with

Built for your setting

For schools

Age-appropriate sessions that help students name their feelings, and equip teachers and parents to notice when a child needs support.

  • Emotional literacy and feelings workshops for students
  • Exam stress, bullying and screen-time sessions
  • Teacher training on spotting early warning signs
  • Parent awareness talks
  • Art-based and letter-writing activities for younger students

For colleges

From orientation week to exam season, we help campuses build a culture where asking for help is normal.

  • Awareness workshops on anxiety, stress and burnout
  • Listening circles and peer-support sessions
  • World Mental Health Day and campus placard campaigns
  • Talks and panel events with student clubs and fests
  • Guided imagery and art therapy experiences
Enquire now

For corporates

Practical sessions that help teams manage stress, prevent burnout and talk openly about wellbeing at work.

  • Stress management and burnout prevention workshops
  • Manager training: having supportive conversations
  • Team listening circles and wellbeing check-ins
  • Mental health awareness weeks and World Mental Health Day events
  • Referral pathways to professional care through R4 Therapy
